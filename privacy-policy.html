Privacy Policy for Charlie

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

At Charlie, we prioritize your privacy and data security. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our AI video generation app.

1. Information We Collect

Charlie is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) from users.

  • Photos/Videos: The images and videos you upload are processed temporarily to generate your video. They are not stored on our servers after processing is complete.
  • Device Data: We may collect basic device information (e.g., OS version, device model) solely for crash reporting and performance optimization. This data is anonymized.
  • No User Accounts: The app does not require login, email, or account creation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for the purpose of generating videos:

  • On-Device Processing: Where possible, processing happens directly on your device.
  • Secure API Calls: When cloud processing is required, your photos are sent via encrypted HTTPS to our AI partners. These partners process the image and return the video. We do not retain a copy of your original photo.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties. Your photos and generated videos remain yours alone.

We use third-party AI services (e.g., Runway, ElevenLabs) for specific generation tasks. These services have their own privacy policies, but we ensure all connections are encrypted and data is not retained by them beyond the processing window.

4. Children's Privacy

Charlie is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data in transit.
  • No persistent storage of user media.
  • Regular security audits of our infrastructure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be communicated via an in-app notification or the app's update notes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of any data we might temporarily hold.
  • Opt-out of non-essential analytics (enabled by default to improve app stability).
