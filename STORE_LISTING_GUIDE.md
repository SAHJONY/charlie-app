## 🚀 Next Steps

1. **Host Privacy Policy:**
   - Upload `privacy-policy.html` to Vercel: `vercel deploy`
   - Get the URL (e.g., `https://charlie-privacy.vercel.app`)
   - Paste it in Play Console under "Privacy Policy URL"

2. **Create Feature Graphic:**
   - Use Canva: Search "Google Play Feature Graphic"
   - Add "Charlie" logo + tagline "Universal AI Video Engine"
   - Export as 1024x500px PNG

3. **Take Screenshots:**
   - Run the app on your phone
   - Capture the 5 screens listed above
   - Ensure text is readable

4. **Submit!**
   - Upload `.aab` file
   - Paste all text fields
   - Upload graphics
   - Submit for review (1-3 days)

---

## 🎯 Pro Tips

- **Launch on Friday:** Apps reviewed on weekends often get approved faster.
- **Internal Testing First:** If you want to test with 10 friends before public launch, use "Internal Testing" track.
- **Update Regularly:** Submit updates every 2-4 weeks to keep the app active in Google's algorithm.

Good luck with your launch! 🎉
